# Code of Conduct

Naatkaati is a shared space for people who care about Tamil language, culture, and good software.

## Expected
- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-developers.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Back data claims with sources.
- Write in Tamil or English, whichever you're comfortable with.

## Not acceptable
- Harassment, personal attacks, or insults.
- Discrimination of any kind, including by caste, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or region.
- Hate speech, or content that demeans any community.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Discussion of contested topics
This project takes a clear product stance (secular, no astrology). Discussing that stance respectfully is welcome; attacking people who hold other beliefs is not.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn, suspend, or ban contributors who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainer: nalinssn@gmail.com.
